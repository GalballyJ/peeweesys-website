1. 100% Drop-In Factory 986.1 Fix .657.02 / .657.03 11-Byte KFPU Retune (0x10F3B)

What it is: The only in-place calibration change Porsche made to the 986.1 Boxster S 3.2L between 91003718 (.657.01) and the final factory revisions 93103708 (.657.02) & 93203708 (.657.03).

Exact bytes: Raises 11 cells in rows 0–1 (2.0%–3.5% throttle) × cols 0–5 (640–2,520 rpm) at 0x10F3B..0x10F52 by +1..+3 raw counts (+0.8%..+2.3% fuel pulsation correction).

Why backport it: Gives you 100% of Porsche's terminal .657.03 calibration refinement without needing a full 512 KB PAF+DAF reflash or moving any map offsets.

2. 986.2 3.2S Overrun Decel Refinement KFNWEGM (0x1964C) — How a Manual Knows What Gear You're In & Burble Impact

How a manual Boxster knows the gear (NO gearbox sensor!): Disassembly at CPU 0x859A84..0x859B8E proves ME7.2 calculates the ratio nvquot_w = nmot_w [0x00F842] / vfz_w [0x38129E] (engine RPM ÷ ABS wheel speed, stored in [0x38125E]) and compares it against the 6-speed NVQUOT1O..6U window table at 0x14F7C..0x14F93 (1st: 85–165, 2nd: 56–85, 3rd: 40.5–56, 4th: 33.5–40.5, 5th: 27.9–33.5, 6th: 21.0–27.9 rpm/(km/h)), writing gear 0..6 to gangi [0x380A25]. When the clutch pedal is pressed (FD32.10 = 1) or stationary (B8DE.0 = 1), it immediately forces gangi = 0!

Complementary or counter-productive to burbles?
• High-RPM lift-off burble (>2,000 rpm via KFTVSA): KFNWEGM does not interfere with lift-off burbles above 2,000 rpm, and lowering in-gear columns 1–3 ([2000, 2000, 1800, 1560] → [1480, 1400, 1320, 1240] rpm) keeps the car smoothly in fuel-cut longer while coasting down in 1st–3rd gear.
• Low-speed town gurgle (<2,000 rpm): The stock town gurgle happens because fuel turns back ON at 2,000 rpm in col 0 (clutch-in) / cols 1–2 (1st/2nd gear) while KFZWMN rows 0–4 are at -1.50°..-8.25° ATDC! If you lower col 0..2 to 1,480 rpm, fuel stays cut from 2,000 down to 1,480 rpm — eliminating the 1,500–2,000 rpm town gurgle. Best of both worlds: keep col 0 (clutch-in) / col 1 (1st gear) at 1,800–2,000 rpm for the town gurgle, and lower cols 2–4 (2nd–4th gear) to 1,400 / 1,320 / 1,240 rpm for smoother in-gear decel!

3. 986.2 3.2S Component Protection KFLBTS (0x19E2F) & TABGBTS (0x1B40E) — Does It Protect a Burble Tune?

What it protects: Exhaust-gas temperature (EGT) component-protection target lambda (KFLBTS, consumed at CPU 0x866838..0x866A42) triggers when modeled exhaust temperature exceeds TABGBTS @ 0x1B40E = 858.5 °C (868.5 °C on 986.2 ME7.8, hysteresis DTABGBTS @ 0x1B40C = 20.0 °C), enriching WOT / high-load mixture to cool the pistons, exhaust valves, and catalytic converters.

Crucial safety distinction — WOT vs. Overrun Burbles:
1. No physical EGT probe: The 986 has no physical EGT thermocouple; tabgm_w is a mathematical software model calculated from engine load, RPM, and ignition retard.
2. Active under WOT/high-load, NOT closed-throttle overrun: KFLBTS only enriches high-load cells (rl >= 60–85%) when you are on the throttle. During closed-throttle overrun burbles (rl ~ 12–15%), dumping extra fuel into the exhaust with retarded spark (ATDC) would actually increase exothermic combustion inside the catalytic converter!
3. What keeps burbles safe for stock cats: Overrun cat safety comes strictly from keeping the KFTVSA (0x1A2B6) fuel-cut delay short (1.0–1.2 s max) and keeping KFZWMN/MS timing mild (-3° to -6° ATDC) so unburned mixture only pops briefly on lift-off before 100% fuel cut cools the cats with fresh air.

4. Stationary Soft Rev Limiter Note NMAXOG (0x1675E) — Why It Is 5,000 RPM on ME7.8

What it is: Upper RPM threshold NMAXOG at 0x1675E (7,200 rpm on our ME7.2 vs 5,000 rpm on 986.2 2.7L & 3.2S ME7.8).

Engineering caution: On ME7.8, program code gates NMAXOG = 5,000 rpm behind a clutch/stationary status bit (B8DE.0) as a neutral rev limiter while driving hard limit stays NMAX = 7,200 rpm. On ME7.2, verify whether NMAXOG is active in gear or only under elevated-limit conditions before lowering it separately from NMAX.

5. Hardware-Coupled Maps — NEVER Copy KRKTE, TVUB, KFNW, KFMSNWDK

Why they differ on 986.2 3.2S ME7.8 (47203728):
• KRKTE (0x19802) & TVUB (0x15E4C): 986.1 3.2S uses KRKTE = 59 (0.1047 ms/%); 986.2 3.2S uses KRKTE = 71 (0.1260 ms/%) with different Bosch EV6 injector dead-times.
• KFNW / KFNWWL: ME7.2 is 2-state on/off VarioCam (0° / 30°); ME7.8 is continuous PWM VarioCam (0°..40°).
• KFMSNWDK / KFWDKMSN: Matched to physical throttle body diameter & manifold pressure geometry.
