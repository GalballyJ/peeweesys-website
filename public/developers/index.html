Open-Source Porsche ME7.2 Disassembly, Definitions & Live Telemetry

Unlike later Porsche ME7.8 ECUs where a factory A2L/DAMOS leaked years ago, no factory A2L exists in the wild for early electronic-throttle Porsche 986 Boxster (2.7 / 3.2S) and 996 Carrera (3.4) Bosch ME7.2 ECUs. This portal hosts our clean-room C167CR disassembly, cross-ECU structural correlation against the ME7.8 3.2S golden reference, live KWP2000 RAM & 0x2C DDLI telemetry maps, and free community WinOLS .A2L & TunerPro .XDF definition files.
