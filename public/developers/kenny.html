2. What We Proved in Disassembly & Live Road Logs

My Earlier AI Red Herring #1 Why I Asked You to Edit KFSU (0x19794) & KFSU2 (0x197C6) — And Why My Old AI Was Wrong!

When we did the MildBurble_KFSU revision, I asked you to change KFSU (0x19794) & KFSU2 (0x197C6) based on what my first AI tool told me at the time. Now that we've disassembled the actual C167 function that reads 0x19794 (CPU 0x861880) and logged it live on the car (Session 4), we proved my earlier AI suggestion was a total red herring: KFSU is Saugrohrumschaltung (the intake manifold resonance flap solenoid) writing RAM word [0x382052]. Lowering its first 3 RPM breakpoints (0x19788..0x1978A) from 2,520 rpm to 1,800 / 1,880 / 1,960 rpm simply makes the intake flap toggle at ~1,960 rpm on decel — it has zero connection to overrun fuel cut or spark! So we should put KFSU/KFSU2 back to stock.

Proven Sign Behavior #2 Zeroing KFZWMN (0x12060) & KFZWMS (0x121E0) Columns 0–1 Locked Spark at 0.00° TDC Instead of Retarding It

In C167 function CPU 0x87C2B6, the ECU looks up KFZWMN (0x12060, blended with KFZWMNX @ 0x12120 into [0x380CED]) and KFZWMS (0x121E0 into [0x380CEE]), then takes min(CED, CEE) → [0x380CEF] → zwout. These are signed bytes (SBYTE × 0.75°), where negative numbers (0xFC = -3.0°, 0xF8 = -6.0°) mean retard after TDC (ATDC) and 0x00 means 0.00° TDC. Setting cols 0–1 in rows 5–15 (2,000–7,200 rpm) to 0x00 locked decel spark at 0.00° TDC instead of retarding it into ATDC.

Proven 1-Byte Selection Slip #3 Byte 0x10339 (0x2E → 0x30) is Inside KFZWOP1 (Row 15, Col 15), Not the Header of KFZWOP2

KFZWOP1 (16×16 SBYTE) runs from 0x1023A..0x10339 and KFZWOP2 runs from 0x1033A..0x10439 (no inline header byte between them). The edit at 0x10339 (+34.50° → +36.00°) nudged the top-right cell (7,200 rpm × 99.75% load) of KFZWOP1 by +1.50° while leaving KFZWOP2's matching cell at 0x10439 at +34.50°.

Verified Smart Edit #4 How Your LAMFA Enrichment Works (0x1B424 Axis + 0x1B430 Table)

We traced the LAMFA consumer at CPU 0x866A4E..0x866ACE: the 12 bytes immediately before LAMFA (0x1B424..0x1B42F) are its 6× 16-bit Little-Endian X-axis (driver requested torque/load %). On an untuned Boxster, the brief 0.25-second dashpot/wall-film transient on snap lift-off and the 0.50 s mid-RPM fuel window (at +2.25°..+3.75° BTDC) are only audible if you fit a straight-through aftermarket backbox — the stock multi-chamber backbox completely muffles them. To make a crisp burble audible through the stock OEM backbox (just like newer-gen Porsche calibrations do), the assembly shows we need both Step A (Negative ATDC Spark) and Step B (Extended Fuel Window in KFTVSA) working together. Does this 2-step recipe match how you'd set it up once the true map locations are exposed?

Step A — Spark Put Negative ATDC Values in KFZWMN & KFZWMS Cols 0–1 (Rows 5–9: 2,000–4,000 rpm)

Right now cols 0–1 (12.0% & 14.2% load — exactly where every road-speed coast lands, median 14.5% load) are 0x00 (0.00° TDC). Because the ECU computes CEF = min(KFZWMN_blended, KFZWMS), setting cols 0–1 in KFZWMN (0x12060) and KFZWMS (0x121E0) to e.g. -3.0° to -6.0° ATDC (0xFC..0xF8 in rows 5–9, 2,000–4,000 rpm) lets zwout retard smoothly past TDC on lift-off so the charge ignites late into the exhaust stroke — staying right inside the stock -8.25° magnitude Porsche already uses in rows 0–4.

Note: Through a stock OEM backbox, Step A needs Step B's fuel window above 2,500 rpm so the retarded spark actually has mixture to pop before fuel cut engages.

Step B — Fuel Window Raise KFTVSA (0x1A2B6) Warm Cells from 0.50 s / 0.00 s to ~1.20–1.50 s (and revert KFSU to Stock)

In stock KFTVSA (0x1A2B6, 5 tmot rows × 8 RPM cols, 0.01 s per count), warm coolant rows (45°C & 50.2°C) give:

• 2,520–3,000 rpm (cols 2–3): 0.50 s (0x32)
