3. Overrun Fuel-Cut Logic, Why the First Burbles File Kangarooed, & Does This AI Recipe Make Sense?

How ME7.2 Overrun Actually Works (High-Level + Firmware Detail):
When you lift off the throttle at speed, the ECU doesn't guess — it runs a strict two-part state machine for Fuel and Spark:

The Complete Overrun Chain Fuel Timer (KFTVSA) + Spark Floor (KFZWMN / KFZWMS)

Plain-English Summary: As soon as you close the throttle above ~1,200 rpm, a countdown timer starts. While the timer is still ticking down, the injectors keep spraying a tiny idle/overrun mist of fuel and the ECU drops ignition timing down to a "minimum timing floor" so the car decelerates smoothly. The instant that timer hits zero, the ECU slams the injectors 100% OFF (pure air pumping through the exhaust) until revs drop back near ~1,900 rpm to catch idle.
C167 Firmware & Live-Log Detail: Closed-throttle detection ([0x3823A2].3 → FD22.3) + mode gate FD48.15 triggers CPU 0x86C540 to load countdown latch A24E / A24D (10 ms tick) from KFTVSA (0x1A2B6, 5×8 UBYTE × 0.01 s). Stock warm cells give 0.90 s (≤2,000 rpm), 0.50 s (2,520–3,000 rpm), and 0.00 s (≥3,520 rpm — instant cut!). When A24D == 0, bit 8BCA.1 sets FD24.10 = 1 (b_sa_cut) and OBD PID 0x03 flips 02 02 → 04 04 as all 4 O2s drop to 0.000–0.020 V. Simultaneously, CPU 0x87C2B6 looks up KFZWMN (0x12060, blended with KFZWMNX @ 0x12120 via [0x3826BC] → CED [0x380CED]) and KFZWMS (0x121E0 → CEE [0x380CEE]), taking CEF = min(CED, CEE) → zwout (0x00F90B). Below 2,000 rpm (rows 0–4), stock KFZWMN is -1.50° to -8.25° ATDC (which is why it naturally gurgles in town when fuel turns back on for idle catch). Above 2,000 rpm, zeroing cols 0–1 locked zwout = 0.00° TDC while KFTVSA still cut fuel immediately.
